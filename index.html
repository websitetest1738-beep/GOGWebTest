What We Do
Complete Land Rover Service

From routine maintenance to complex engine and suspension repairs — all performed by specialists who know these vehicles inside and out.

01
Engine Bay Repairs

Vacuum pump replacement, valve cover repairs, and full powertrain service to factory tolerances. We diagnose issues before they become failures.

02
Coolant System Repairs

Outlet pipe, radiator, thermostat, and rear coolant manifold. Florida’s heat demands a reliable cooling system — we keep yours right.

03
Air Suspension Repair

Compressors, air springs, height sensors, and terrain response calibration. We restore proper ride height using Land Rover-specific tools.

04
Diagnostics

OEM-level scan tools built for Land Rover systems. We find root causes — not just clear codes. No guesswork, no unnecessary repairs.

05
Routine Maintenance

Synthetic oil changes, filter replacements, fluid checks, and factory-scheduled service intervals performed to manufacturer specifications.

06
Brakes, A/C & More

Complete brake service, ABS calibration, A/C repair, battery replacement, belts and hoses, alternator, starter, and transmission service.
